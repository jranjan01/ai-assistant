ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance.

SICK LEAVE
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

CASUAL LEAVE
Employees get 10 days of casual leave per calendar year.
Casual leave can be taken for personal matters or emergencies.
Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.
Employees should inform their manager before taking casual leave.

MATERNITY LEAVE
Eligible female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Employees should notify HR at least 30 days before the planned maternity leave.
A medical certificate may be required to support the leave request.

PATERNITY LEAVE
Eligible male employees are entitled to 15 days of paid paternity leave.
Paternity leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
Employees must inform HR before applying for paternity leave.

PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Employees are entitled to 12 public holidays per calendar year.
Public holidays are separate from annual leave.
The company publishes the holiday calendar at the beginning of each year.

LEAVE APPROVAL
All planned leave requests must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.
Employees must submit leave requests through the HR management system.
Emergency leave requests should be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

LEAVE CANCELLATION
Managers can request employees to reschedule leave due to urgent business requirements.
Employees can cancel approved leave by informing their reporting manager.
Cancelled leave will be credited back to the employee's leave balance.

UNPAID LEAVE
Employees may request unpaid leave if their paid leave balance is exhausted.
Unpaid leave requires approval from the department manager and HR.
The salary will be adjusted based on the number of unpaid leave days.
