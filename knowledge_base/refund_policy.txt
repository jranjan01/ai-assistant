MONTHLY PLANS
Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime. Refunds are available within 7 days of the first payment only. No refunds for later months.

ANNUAL PLANS
Annual plans are refundable within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are prorated and a 10% processing fee applies.
 
HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND
Email billing@cloud.example with your account ID and invoice number. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.
